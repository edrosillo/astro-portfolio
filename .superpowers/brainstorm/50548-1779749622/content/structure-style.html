Without heavy borders, how should sections feel separated?

We still need visual structure — just not brutalist thick borders. Which approach feels right?

A

Generous Whitespace

Let breathing room do the work. Large gaps between sections, no visible dividers. Clean, confident, magazine-editorial feel. Structure comes from typography scale and spacing alone.

B

Thin Lines + Section Numbers

Subtle 1px lines between sections with monospace labels like "001 — FEATURED WORK". Light and structured without feeling heavy. The numbering adds a retro-technical layer.

C

Alternating Background Tones

Alternate between pure white and a very subtle off-white or light gray (#f5f5f5). Sections feel distinct without any lines at all. Cards and elements float on the surface.

D

Mix — Whitespace + Thin Accents

Mostly generous whitespace, with occasional thin accent lines or subtle dividers where needed. No strict system — use what feels right per section. Flexible and natural.

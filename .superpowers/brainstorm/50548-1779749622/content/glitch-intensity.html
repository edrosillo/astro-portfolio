How aggressive should the glitch effects be?

You love glitchiness — but there's a spectrum. Where should this portfolio sit?

A

Subtle — Glitch as Accent

Chromatic aberration on hover only. Occasional flicker on page transitions. The site feels clean 95% of the time — glitch is a surprise that rewards interaction. Professional first, playful second.

B

Moderate — Glitch as Texture

Static chromatic aberration on hero name. Glitch animations trigger on scroll and hover. Scanline overlays on images. VHS tracking on page transitions. The glitch is always present but controlled — part of the visual DNA.

C

Bold — Glitch as Identity

Hero name constantly glitching with animated RGB split. Random glitch bursts across sections. Distortion on scroll. CRT flicker on page load. The whole site feels like it's alive and slightly unstable — controlled chaos that shows mastery.

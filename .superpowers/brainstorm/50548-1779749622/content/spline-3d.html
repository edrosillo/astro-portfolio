What should happen with the Spline 3D hero scene?

The 3D scene is your current hero's biggest visual element. With the switch to a light theme, we need to decide how it fits.

A

Keep It — Adapt to Light Theme

Restyle the Spline scene to work on white. Adjust the canvas background, lighting, and colors. It's already a strong piece — just needs to match the new palette.

B

Replace with Custom Hero Animation

Drop the third-party Spline embed and build a custom hero animation — maybe a glitch-text reveal, particle system, or geometric animation. Shows you built it yourself, which is more impressive to employers.

C

Keep It But Make It Secondary

Move the 3D scene to be smaller or a background element. Let the typography and glitch effects be the hero's star. The 3D adds depth without dominating.

D

Remove It Entirely

Go pure typography + glitch for the hero. No 3D, no embed. Bold text, chromatic aberration, maybe a subtle dot-grid or geometric background. Faster load time, fully custom, maximum impact from simplicity.

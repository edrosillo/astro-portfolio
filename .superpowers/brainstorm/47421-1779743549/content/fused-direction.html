OVERALL SYSTEM: The design language is defined by: (1) thick black borders as primary structural element, (2) massive uppercase type with tight letter-spacing, (3) monospace labels for section numbers and metadata, (4) dot-grid subtle background texture, (5) cyan + pink used only for accent moments — glitch layers, select skill bars, hover states. Everything else is black and white. The restraint makes the color hits land harder.
